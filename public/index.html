Architecture & Compliance Blueprint

Engineering Decisions & Source Boundaries

Analysis of detection surfaces, public API sourcing, resilience tradeoffs, and legal boundaries.

01

Detection Surface in 2026

Anti-bot defenses no longer rely on naive User-Agent or `navigator.webdriver` flags. Real defenses inspect cross-signal consistency.

  • Cross-Signal Consistency: A UA claiming Windows Chrome while WebGL reports SwiftShader/llvmpipe virtual GPU is an instant tell.
  • Hardware-Level Fingerprints: Cloud VM GPUs are trivially distinguishable from consumer hardware; spoofing is compute-expensive at scale.
  • Network-Layer Fingerprints (TLS/JA4): Requires transport-level patching, catching off-the-shelf scrapers automatically.
  • Behavioral Biometrics: Micro-acceleration and natural cursor jitter vs scripted linear math.
  • Adaptive Blocking: Thresholds evolve dynamically; absence of an immediate ban is not evidence of undetectability.
02

Tiered Sourcing Strategy

The core strategic rule: Don't scrape what is already syndicated.

  • Tier 1 - Direct ATS APIs: Greenhouse is live, with Lever and Ashby-compatible parser support for public board payloads.
  • Tier 2 - Licensed/Partner Feeds: RemoteOK, Arbeitnow, and Jobicy expose public APIs designed for programmatic consumption.
  • Tier 3 - Design Only: Hostile aggregators have no live source entry, parser, cache seed, or re-enable toggle in this repo.
  • Incremental Fetching: Updated-at watermarks skip already-seen records while Bronze still preserves raw payloads.
  • Last-Good Cache Fallback: Public API snapshots are cached only after real successful runs.
03

Resilience & Data Quality

Nothing in this pipeline fails silently. A malformed response is caught; a schema change is loud.

  • Retry & Circuit Breaking: Exponential backoff with jitter handles transient failures; repeated failures open a source-level circuit.
  • Schema Contracts & DLQ: Canonical Zod validation catches drift, while malformed records are retained in a dead-letter queue.
  • Entity Resolution Bands: Exact hashes dedupe automatically; high-confidence fuzzy matches merge; ambiguous matches enter review.
04

Legal & Ethical Boundaries

Grounded in modern 9th Circuit case law rather than vague compliance gestures.

  • Public, Logged-Out Data Only: hiQ v. LinkedIn and Meta v. Bright Data inform the public-data boundary used here.
  • Zero Auth Bypass: No fake accounts, credential stuffing, or bypassing login walls.
  • No Commercial Resale of Personal Data: Prevents breach-of-contract liability and privacy regulatory exposure.
  • Rule of Thumb: If data isn't reachable without login, find the licensed partner API instead of trying to hide deeper.
